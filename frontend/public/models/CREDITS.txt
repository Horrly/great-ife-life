People models and animations in this folder

person-m.glb, person-f.glb and person-anims.glb are built from these packs by Quaternius
(https://quaternius.com), all released under CC0 1.0 (public domain):
  - Universal Base Characters (Standard)
  - Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy (Standard)
  - Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2 (Standard)

Clothes are retinted, hair is recoloured, textures are resized to WebP, and the animation
clips are trimmed to the ones the game uses. Thank you, Quaternius.
